Weber Marine Conversion Kits

We now offer performance style Weber marine Conversion Kits for the applications listed below. These Kits deliver faster and easier staring, smoother idle and low speed operation, increased low - end torque, grater horsepower and acceleration, and more top - end RPM's and range. These are great!


For Mercuisers :

 

Kits will fit 4-cylinder and 4.3 liter V-6 engines that originally came equipped with a 2-barrel Mercarb marine carburetor. Each Kit includes a brand new 2-barrel Weber performance carburetor with electric choke, flame arrestor, mounting hardware, fuel line and fittings, and throttle linkage parts


For 4-Cyl. Volvo Penta:

Kits will fit later production "slant 4" Volvo - B-21 Penta marine motors, and earlier B-18 and B-20 motors that came equipped with 1-barrel Solex marine carburetors or Zenith Strombergs. Each kit includes a new intake manifold, to convert to a single carburetor, plus a brand new 2-barrel Weber performance carburetor with electric choke, flame arrestor, mounting hardware, fuel line and fittings, and throttle linkage parts. For the B-18 and B-20 motors, it is a " you make it kit " Some simple adapting will always be needed.
