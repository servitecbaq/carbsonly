Weber Carburetors

Weber carburetors are world-famous for performance and exceptional tune ability. They've been proven around the world under all sorts of conditions, on streets and highways, thousands of race tracks, and the toughest off road environments. Now Webers can be installed on more cars and trucks than ever before, thanks to the dedication and quality engineering behind each Redline Weber Conversion Kit. And now we also offer the Weber Marine Conversion Kits for certain marine applications.

At BFIC we feature all of the Redline Weber Conversion Kits, the best engineered and most complete available. These Kits are built with all brand new parts. Each Kit is engineered specifically for its application, the carburetors are pre-jetted and calibrated, and the Kits are backed by excellent technical support. This means great out-of-the-box performance, and a solid baseline for any tuning that might be needed later. Typically Redline Weber Kits will deliver more torque, greater horsepower, improved throttle response, smoother operation and better overall performance. When used off-road, Webers will provide consistent fuel flow at nearly any angle. Jeep and Trucks are now available with manual choke Webers. We also feature all of the new Webers carburetors, sold separately, plus jets and floats, linkage and repair parts, air cleaners and accessories, and excellent " Weber Tuning Manual." And we rebuild all types of the Webers, in our own facility.

Available new Webers include the 34 ICT; 34 ICH; 32/36 DGV; 32/36 DGEV; 32/36 DFEV; the "outlaw performance" 38 DGAS synchronous two-barrel; the outstanding 40, 44 & 48 IDF two barrel down-draft carburetors, and the world famous 40, 45, 48, 50 & 55 DCOE & DCO side-drafts. Plus we have brand new Weber 40 DCNF's. Available while supplies last
