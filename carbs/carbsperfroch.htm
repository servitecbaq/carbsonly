Performance Rochesters

We're pleased to offer custom-built Performance Quads and 2G two-barrel Rochesters for a range of street performance, off-road and racing applications.

Every one of our custom Rochesters is built expressly for the engine, the application, and the driving requirements, so there's no "one size fit's all." You'll need to give us the information we require in order to specify and build the carburetor correctly, for your non-emissions car or truck. Once you contact us, we always "interview" our customers, by phone or in person, and we'll ask a number of questions before starting the work

 

We can build your Rochester core, or sell you one of ours. We have genuine "big-bore" 2G cores in stock, and quads in several sizes. Our Performance Rochesters are designed for:

• Street performance - mild to wild -- great for "built" non- emissions motors in cars and trucks.
• Off-road applications
• High altitude
• All types of racing
